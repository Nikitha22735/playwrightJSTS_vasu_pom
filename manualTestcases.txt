TC-01: Verify search results page loads for a valid keyword

Objective:
Validate that a valid search term shows relevant product results on the Amazon results screen.

Preconditions:
- User is on the Amazon.in homepage.
- Internet connectivity is available.

Steps:
1. Open https://www.amazon.in/
2. Enter the keyword "iphone" in the search box.
3. Click the Search button.
4. Wait until the results page loads.

Expected Result:
- The page URL changes to a search results page.
- The result summary text is displayed, for example: "1-16 of over 50,000 results for \"iphone\"".
- At least one product card is visible on the page.
- The page title contains the searched keyword.

Actual observation on the live app:
- Search for "iphone" returned a results page with the result count text visible and the sort dropdown visible.

TC-02: Verify sorting results by price (Low to High)

Objective:
Validate that the user can sort displayed products from lowest price to highest price.

Preconditions:
- User is on the Amazon results page for "iphone".

Steps:
1. Open Amazon.in and search for "iphone".
2. Locate the Sort By dropdown.
3. Select "Price: Low to High".
4. Wait for the products to refresh.

Expected Result:
- The selected sort option is updated to "Price: Low to High".
- The product list reorders according to lowest price first.
- The first visible product has the lowest price among the current result set.
- The page remains stable and no error message is shown.

Actual observation on the live app:
- The results page displays a visible Sort By section and supports sorting options such as Featured and Price: Low to High.
